# Privacy Policy for Cultonomics

This Privacy Policy informs you of our policies about the information collected when you use the Service. By using the Service, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this Privacy Policy.

### The information we collect

We collect certain information from our users, this information is securely saved and protected.

The information collected includes:
- Discord user ID, when you join a push, buy or sell an item in a channel the Bot can see, or use a Bot command.
- Discord server, channel and message IDs related to the above, and the channel and role settings a server sets up for the Bot.
- IDLE FARM purchases and sales the Bot sees in IDLE FARM's replies: item, amount, cost and day.
- IDLE FARM market prices and item stock status the Bot sees in IDLE FARM's replies.

The Bot reads messages in channels it has access to only to recognise IDLE FARM's buy, sell and market replies. It does not store message text, usernames or any other content of your messages.

### How we use your information

This information is used for the functionality of the Bot, such as announcing pushes, tracking who joined a push and how much they bought, sending reminders and sell calls, and keeping logs for the team that runs the Bot.

Push progress (for example, who still has to buy) may be shown by mentioning you in the Bot's messages in the servers that use it.
This collected information is not sold or shared with anyone else.
